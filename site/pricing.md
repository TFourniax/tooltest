# Proposed DiffWitness commercial-alpha plans

The pricing shown on /pricing/ is a design proposal, not an announced public offer.

- Community: €0. Local Protect, Proof, certificates, Debt Ledger, deterministic IdleProof and user-owned inference choices.
- Builder: proposed €19/month. Guided Portal, history, readiness and 500 managed explanations/month.
- Pro: proposed €39/month. Multiple projects, advanced longitudinal views and 1,200 managed explanations/month.
- Team: proposed €29/user/month. Shared visibility, PR/CI coordination, roles and a finite per-seat managed-AI pool.

Annual prices shown in the mockup represent a proposed 20% discount.

The public runner remains proof authority on every tier. Paid plans add convenience and coordination, not stronger truth labels.
