These prices are the proposed commercial-alpha structure for this site mockup, not an announced public offer. Product entitlements and finite managed-AI quotas reflect the current architecture; final pricing requires a separate commercial decision before publication.

Where the boundary sits

Do not paywall the truth. Charge for leverage.

The paid layer makes the same truth easier to consume across time, projects and people.

FREE CORE

Local Protect, executable Proof, certificates, Debt Ledger and deterministic explanation.

PAID UX

Guided Portal, longitudinal history, readiness and cross-project presentation.

PAID AI

Finite managed explanation quotas with reservation, retry and spend breakers.

NEVER

No “unlimited” inference and no silent paid fallback for a free or BYOK user.
