The visual thesis

Complexity is not the enemy. Unseen complexity is.

The Sphaira is not a product simulation or a live view of your repository. It is the brand’s central metaphor: change creates local disorder; an independent witness does not erase that change, but makes its consequences inspectable.

WHITE · ARRIVING

New agent-generated surface enters the system faster than a person can review it.

GREY · DRIFTING

Unexplained interactions and accumulated obligations reduce legibility over time.

VIOLET · WITNESSED

Evidence binds a bounded claim to the exact change. It does not claim universal certainty.
