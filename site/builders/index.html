The questions you should not need to invent

From “it works” to “here is what we know.”

DiffWitness turns the hidden review burden into four explicit moments.

1

What is the agent trying to do?

during the session

Optional Protect watches supported dangerous actions and either records, pauses or blocks them under the policy you chose.

2

Did the change fix anything?

after the session

DiffWitness checks whether the new evidence actually distinguishes the changed version from the old one.

3

Which edits were truly involved?

before acceptance

It removes real pieces of the patch in controlled variants and observes what the evidence does.

4

What do I still owe?

after merge

Unresolved shortcuts remain visible until the matching repayment is independently checked.

Agent session controlsproject setting
  • CONTINUE
    Rename a checkout label

    No high-confidence Protect rule matched. Your agent’s own permissions remain in control.

  • REVIEW
    Install a new dependency

    Strict policy asks for confirmation where the provider supports it.

  • BLOCKED
    Force-clean the repository

    A high-confidence destructive operation matched the active policy.

Protect is a choice

A seat belt you can configure. Not another driver.

Use DiffWitness protection, keep the safety harness you already trust, or disable live interception completely. The post-change check and project history keep working in every mode.

Important: a blocked command is an observed runtime event. It is never presented as proof that the final application works correctly.

Switch at any time

Useful without another AI bill

The facts do not come from a second model’s opinion.

The local explanation is deterministic. Optional AI can improve the wording, but it cannot add evidence or upgrade uncertainty.

  • FREEDeterministic local explanation. No model, network or DiffWitness-paid call.
  • YOURSReuse your active coding-agent session, a local model, OpenRouter/BYOK or a custom endpoint.
  • MANAGEDEligible paid plans add finite managed-explanation quotas with hard cost boundaries.
  • FALLBACKWhen any provider fails or quota ends, the deterministic explanation remains available.
You do not need to read every line. You need a system that never hides what it does not know.
