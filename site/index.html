The assurance loop

One instrument. Four moments.

Not four disconnected products. One loop before, during and after a software change.

1

Protect

while the agent works · optional

Block high-confidence dangerous actions, observe them, delegate to your harness, or leave interception off.

2

Witness

before you accept

Run controlled counterfactuals on the real patch and the executable evidence you selected.

3

Explain

when you ask

Translate bounded facts into plain language locally, through your model, or with managed AI on eligible plans.

4

Remember

after merge, for good

Keep accepted obligations alive, rank the next repayment and verify whether the debt was actually repaid.

Trust vocabulary

Uncertainty remains visible.

Plain language can change. The evidence class underneath it cannot.

VERIFIED

Established by an executed verification adapter for the bound change.

OBSERVED

Present in bounded project or runtime metadata, but not causal proof.

SUGGESTED

A useful next action or question. Recommendation is not evidence.

UNKNOWN

The selected evidence cannot support a stronger statement yet.

Debt Ledger

Accepted risk should not disappear after merge.

Git remembers what happened. It does not reliably preserve why a shortcut was accepted, what would repay it, or whether that repayment was later proved.
