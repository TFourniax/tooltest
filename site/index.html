What we claim, and what we don't.

The public runner remains the verification authority. The private planner may prioritize counterfactual experiments, but it is advisory, never authoritative.

  • we claimProof claims come from executed evidence on the bound Git change. No presentation model may upgrade OBSERVED, SUGGESTED or UNKNOWN into VERIFIED.
  • we claimProtect, Proof and Debt accounting require no model API. The core is local-first and does not require source-code upload.
  • we don'tThat the planner saves 20% of experiments in production. It clears that bar on our controlled benchmark corpus with zero recovered-core mismatch; real-repository measurement remains a separate release gate.
  • we don'tThat DiffWitness replaces review. It narrows what the evidence establishes, exposes unknowns, and keeps unresolved obligations visible.

Accepted risk shouldn't disappear after merge.

Git keeps what happened. It does not keep why you accepted it. The ledger does, and it keeps counting.
